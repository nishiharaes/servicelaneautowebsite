Our Story

Where we began

Service Lane Auto was created with a simple goal: to provide drivers in Condell Park and South West Sydney with a reliable local mechanic they can trust.

We saw the need for a mechanic in Condell Park that combined quality workmanship with honest advice, clear communication and straightforward service. Whether someone was searching for a “mechanic near me,” “car service near me,” brake repairs, car diagnostics or general vehicle maintenance, we wanted Service Lane Auto to be the workshop they could rely on.

From routine car servicing and filter changes to brake repairs, diagnostics and mechanical repairs, our focus has always been on keeping our customers’ vehicles safe, reliable and on the road.

Based in Condell Park, NSW, Service Lane Auto proudly serves drivers and businesses throughout Bankstown, Yagoona, Revesby, Padstow, Milperra and surrounding South West Sydney suburbs.
